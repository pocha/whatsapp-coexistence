Know your limits

  • Messages you start: up to 250 unique customers per 24 hours (rolling window), rising to 2,000 once your business is verified. You can only send these with an approved template, and you pay a per-message cost based on the customer's country.
  • Replies to incoming messages: you can send free form messages for 24 hours after a customer messages you. Messages in this window are not free, apart from the monthly quota below.
    Exception: Any customer starting to message from 'Click to Whatsapp' ad on Instagram/Facebook or by clicking 'chat on whatsapp' CTA on your facebook page, you can respond to them for free for upto 7 days, provided you have responded to the incoming message within 24 hours at least once.
  • Free monthly quota: each business phone number gets 1,000 free replies to incoming messages in a calendar month. After that, the 'service message' cost applies.

Full details on WhatsApp's pricing page. These are Meta's rules, and they can change.
