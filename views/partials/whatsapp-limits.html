Know your limits

  • Conversations you start: up to 250 unique customers per 24 hours (rolling window), rising to 2,000 once your business is verified. You can only send messages with an approved template, and you pay a per-message cost based on the customer's country. There is no free monthly quota for these messages.
  • Conversation started by your customer: you can send free form messages for 24 hours after a customer messages you. You get 1000 free responses per month. After the limit, you are charged at 'service' template rate, which is same as utility message.
    Exception: Any customer starting conversation via 'Click to Whatsapp' ad on Instagram/Facebook or by clicking 'chat on whatsapp' CTA on your facebook page, you can respond to them for free for upto 7 days, provided your first response to them was within 24 hour window of they messaging you.

Full details on WhatsApp's pricing page. These are Meta's rules, and they can change.
