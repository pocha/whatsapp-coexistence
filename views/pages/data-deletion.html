Data Deletion

This app holds WhatsApp Business Account access tokens in memory only, for the duration of onboarding (or, while test features are enabled, for the life of the running server process) — see the project README for details. Nothing is written to a database or disk, so there is normally nothing persisted to delete.

If you'd like us to confirm deletion, or believe we hold data about you that isn't covered above, email developer@shristigroup.com with your WhatsApp Business Account ID and we'll respond within 30 days.

You can also revoke this app's access to your account at any time from Meta Business Manager → Business Settings → Integrations → Apps.
