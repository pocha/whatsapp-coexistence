Data Deletion

We hold your sign-in phone number, the WhatsApp number and WABA IDs you onboarded, the incoming-message URL you set, usage counts, and the access token Meta gave us for each number, stored encrypted (see our Privacy Policy).

To delete all of it yourself, sign in to the Dashboard and choose Delete account in the sidebar. This removes your numbers, usage counts and stored tokens, and your sign-in record. To also cut off our access on Meta's side, remove this app from your business in Meta Business Manager.

If you'd like us to confirm deletion, or believe we hold data about you that isn't covered above, email developer@shristigroup.com with your WhatsApp Business Account ID and we'll respond within 30 days.

You can also revoke this app's access to your account at any time from Meta Business Manager → Business Settings → Integrations → Apps.
