How to De-register Your WhatsApp Number from a Tech Provider or BSP

Another provider's signup can refuse your number with "already registered" while the old provider still holds it. You don't need to be stuck: you can free it yourself. How depends on one thing, whether the number is also on the WhatsApp Business app.

Which one do you have?

  • API only: the number works only through the provider. It's not on the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. Go to API-only numbers.
  • Coexistence: you still use the number in the WhatsApp Business app, and the provider is connected alongside it. Go to coexistence numbers.

Not sure? If you can open the number's chats in the WhatsApp Business app, it's coexistence. The move guide shows how to check in Business Settings.

API-only numbers

There are two ways. Pick the first if you're happy to wait for the provider, the second if you want it done now.

Way 1. Ask the provider to deregister it

  1. Write to the provider's support and ask them to deregister the phone number (their API has a deregister call for it). Say which number and which account.
  2. Ask them to confirm when it's done.
  3. Wait a few minutes, then run the new provider's signup again.

This works whenever the provider is responsive. It doesn't need your PIN, and it's not blocked by recent paid messages.

Way 2. Delete the number yourself in WhatsApp Manager

  1. Open WhatsApp Manager and load your business portfolio. If it opens the wrong account or shows an error, go through Business Settings → Accounts → WhatsApp accounts → your account instead.
  2. Go to Account tools (the toolbox icon) → Phone numbers.
  3. Click the trash can icon next to the number.
  4. Enter the two-step verification PIN when asked, and complete the flow.
  5. Wait a few minutes, then run the new provider's signup again.

What has to be true

  • You must be an admin of the business portfolio that owns the account.
  • The number must not have sent paid messages in the last 30 days. If it has, WhatsApp Manager shows the date of the last paid message instead, and you can delete the number 30 days after it.
  • Deleting can only be done in WhatsApp Manager. Meta doesn't allow it through the API.

Deleting removes the number from that WhatsApp account altogether, which is a stronger step than the provider deregistering it. If you only want to hand the number to another provider, Way 1 is the lighter choice.

If you've lost the PIN

Two-step verification protects the number, and Meta asks for the PIN to change it or to delete the number. Ask the old provider first: many set it and can tell you. If they can't, you can turn it off yourself:

  1. In WhatsApp Manager, go to Account tools → Phone numbers and select the number.
  2. Open the Two-step verification tab and click Change PIN.
  3. On the last step click Turn off two-step verification.
  4. Meta emails a link to the address on your business portfolio. Use it to confirm. Once it's off, you can set a new PIN whenever you like.

The provider can also set a new PIN through the API. You can't turn two-step verification off through the API, only in WhatsApp Manager.

Coexistence numbers

A coexistence number isn't deregistered. It stays registered on your WhatsApp Business app. You only disconnect the provider's API connection. Meta's deregister call can't be used on numbers that are on both.

  1. On your phone, open the WhatsApp Business app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Business Platform.
  3. Select the connected provider and tap Disconnect.
  4. Wait 15 minutes, then run the new provider's signup.

The provider is told the connection was removed, and its sends stop working from that moment. If you then want to leave the number API-free altogether, you're done. If you're moving to another provider, the move guide covers the rest.

What does not deregister a number

  • Removing the provider in Business Settings (unsharing the account or removing the partner) only takes away their access. The number may still be registered, and the next provider's signup can still say "already registered".
  • Cancelling the provider's subscription doesn't deregister it either. Ask them to deregister first, then cancel.
  • Deleting the provider's app or token on your side doesn't touch Meta's registration.

After it's free

  • Wait a few minutes before retrying. Meta's error messages say it can take up to 3 minutes, and 15 minutes after a Business app disconnect.
  • The new provider registers the number with a PIN. If two-step verification is still on, it needs the existing PIN. Otherwise any six digits are used. Keep the PIN safe, and don't give it out.
  • If the signup still says "already registered", the number may be on another WhatsApp product, or in someone else's account. Check the number was typed correctly.
  • Your templates are on the Messaging account, not the number. See the move guide for what to check.
