How to Create Meta Business Portfolio & Add Your WhatsApp Number to It

IS THIS MANDATORY?

Yes, you need one. But you do not need a verified business: create the portfolio with the name of your app or website, and it can stay unverified. Meta's own docs are explicit: "You must have a business portfolio to use the [WhatsApp Business] platform." There's no way to create a WhatsApp-enabled Meta App without connecting it to one. (Source: About the WhatsApp Business Platform.)

1. Create a business portfolio

Go to business.facebook.com and create a new business portfolio. You do not need a different Facebook account. One Facebook account can own several portfolios, so if you land in an existing one (for example, one made for another project), stay logged in and add a new portfolio using the switcher at the top left.

The portfolio switcher (top left) has a "Create a business portfolio" button.

2. Fill in the details

Enter the business details Meta asks for, such as the business name, your name and a business email. If you have a registered business, provide the exact details (name, address and phone number) as on the document you plan to submit for business verification. Later you can edit the display name from Business Settings → Business Info.

3. Open Business Settings

Once the portfolio is created, click the Business Settings button, or go straight to business.facebook.com/latest/settings. If you land on a different account, switch to the new portfolio using the portfolio switcher at the top left.

4. Add your WhatsApp account

First, install the WhatsApp Business app (free on the app store) and log in with the phone number you want the API activated on. Then come back here. In Business Settings, go to Accounts → WhatsApp accounts, click Add and choose Link a WhatsApp Business account.

5. Add an app

Your portfolio needs a Meta app before you can get an access token. See How to Get WhatsApp API for Free.

Your new portfolio, ready to have an app connected to it.
