How to Get WhatsApp Business API Access

Getting WhatsApp Business API access means passing Meta's App Review. There are two things you need in place first:

1. A Business Portfolio

The verified (or verification-eligible) Meta Business Portfolio your app connects to.

2. A Meta App

Created on developers.facebook.com with the WhatsApp product and your App ID / Secret / Config ID.

With both of those in place, here's how App Review itself works.

App Review requires two demo videos

Meta's reviewers need to see your app actually using the whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management permissions:

  • Video 1 — a message created and sent from your app, arriving in the WhatsApp client on the recipient's phone.
  • Video 2 — your app creating a message template, and that template showing up in WhatsApp Manager.

A pure onboarding-only app can't produce these on its own, which is why this project's Dashboard ships small "test" buttons — send a message, create a template — solely to record these two videos. They're not part of the coexistence flow itself; the dashboard's own page explains how to use them for recording, and the project README has the exact click-by-click steps.

Keep it simple

Reviewers are checking that the flow (login → action → result) is real and complete, not evaluating UI polish. Record both videos simply and unedited.

After approval

Once approved, you can turn the test buttons off — they hold your WABA access token in memory to work, which is more exposure than the coexistence flow itself needs. That flow discards the access token immediately after subscribing to your WABA.
