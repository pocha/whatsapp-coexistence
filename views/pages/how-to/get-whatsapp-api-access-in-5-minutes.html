How to Get WhatsApp API Access in 5 minutes

Already have WhatsApp API access through another provider or your own app? Read how to migrate to Watobot first.

You don't need your own Meta app or developer account. We're the Tech Provider, so signing in through us connects your number to the API, and you keep using the WhatsApp Business app on it as before.

Set up a business portfolio first

WhatsApp API access lives in a Meta business portfolio, and you need one even if you don't have a registered business. It can stay unverified. Our guide walks you through creating it and adding your WhatsApp number: How to Create a Meta Business Portfolio & Add Your WhatsApp Number.

Before you start

  • The WhatsApp Business app installed, with your phone number registered on it. Meta requires app version 2.24.17 or higher for coexistence.
  • An app (a chatbot, for example) hosted at a public HTTPS URL that receives incoming messages. You'll give us this URL in step 3, and use the same app to test sending through our endpoint. If you do not have an app yet, you can setup a test-chatbot

1. Connect it from the Dashboard

Sign in to the Dashboard with your WhatsApp number and choose Onboard a WABA. A Facebook popup walks you through connecting the number, and you'll pick your business portfolio there. This creates your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).

2. Save your access token

We show you an access token for your WABA once. We don't store it, so copy it into your app's secrets right away. You'll use it to send messages.

3. Point incoming messages at your app

Open your number's page from the Dashboard and enter your app's incoming-message webhook URL, along with your access token. We check it responds correctly, then route your WhatsApp messages straight to it.

4. Test both directions

  • Send a message to your number from another phone. It should reach your app.
  • From your app, send a message through our endpoint with your access token. The recipient's phone should receive it.

5. Know your messaging limits

Every new business portfolio starts at 250 unique customers per 24-hour period, regardless of verification status. To scale up:

  • Reach 2,000 by either verifying your business, or by sending 2,000 delivered messages outside customer-service windows with high-quality templates within 30 days.
  • Beyond 2,000, limits scale automatically (to 10,000 / 100,000 / unlimited) as long as you keep messaging quality high and use at least 50% of your current limit in the past 7 days.

To verify, follow the business portfolio guide. Full details: Messaging Limits — Meta for Developers.
