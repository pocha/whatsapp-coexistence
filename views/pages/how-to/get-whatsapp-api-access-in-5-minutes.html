How to Get WhatsApp API Access in 5 minutes

Already have WhatsApp API access through another provider or your own app? Read how to move your number first.

You don't need your own Meta app or developer account. We're the Tech Provider, so signing in through us connects your number to the API, and you keep using the WhatsApp Business app on it as before.

Set up a business portfolio first

WhatsApp API access lives in a Meta business portfolio, and you need one even if you don't have a registered business. It can stay unverified. Our guide walks you through creating it and adding your WhatsApp number: How to Create a Meta Business Portfolio & Add Your WhatsApp Number.

Before you start

  • The WhatsApp Business app installed, with your phone number registered on it. Meta requires app version 2.24.17 or higher for coexistence.
  • An app (a chatbot, for example) hosted at a public HTTPS URL that receives what WhatsApp sends: incoming messages, the messages you type in the WhatsApp Business app, and the contacts and chat history Meta syncs once when you connect. Store them if you want that history. If you do not have an app yet, you can setup a test-chatbot.

Rather have your AI tool guide you? Claude, ChatGPT and Claude Code can do the checks and walk you through the same steps: connect your AI tool.

1. Sign in

Sign in to the Dashboard with your WhatsApp number. We send you a one-time code on WhatsApp.

2. Create your API key

The first time, the dashboard sends you to the API key page. We create your Watobot API key in your browser and show it once. Copy it somewhere safe, such as your app's secrets. We keep only a hash of it, so we can't show it again or recover it. We then sign you out, and you'll need the key to sign in again.

3. Onboard your number

Sign in again, choose Onboard Phone Number and then Onboard in the dashboard. A window takes you through it:

  1. Your API key. Enter it. We encrypt the access token Meta gives us with it, so only you can unlock it.
  2. Your callback URL. Enter your app's incoming-message URL. We check it answers Meta's handshake, then send it a test message, an echo, contacts and history, each with a code. Type the four codes your app received, so you know it stores what Meta will send. You can skip that data test. And if you only send messages and reply in the WhatsApp Business app, you can onboard with no callback URL at all, but then no incoming messages or delivery receipts reach an app of yours, and the contacts and history sync is skipped for good, since Meta allows it only once.
  3. The WhatsApp Business app. Confirm your number is on it.
  4. Your templates. Say where they are: none, in your previous Messaging account, or in an export from your provider.
  5. Meta's sign-up. A Facebook popup connects the number, and you pick your business portfolio there. We then point Meta at your URL, so messages go straight from WhatsApp to your app.
  6. Sync, or register. A coexistence number requests its contacts and chat history, which Meta allows once each, within 24 hours (you can also skip it, for good). A number that isn't on the app can carry on as API-only and is registered with a six-digit PIN.
  7. Templates. We copy them across, or import your export.

4. Test both directions

  • Send a message to your number from another phone. It should reach your app.
  • From your app, send a message through our endpoint with Authorization: Bearer <your Watobot API key>. The recipient's phone should receive it.
