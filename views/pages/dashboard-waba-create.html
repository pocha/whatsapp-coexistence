1. WHERE SHOULD YOUR DATA GO?

Right after onboarding, Meta sends your contacts and past chats (once, and it can't be repeated) to this URL, and every message and echo after that. Watobot doesn't keep any of it, so your endpoint must parse and save it. Meta gives 24 hours to request that sync, so we check your endpoint first.

2. IS THE NUMBER ON THE WHATSAPP BUSINESS APP?

Linked devices (WhatsApp Web and so on) are unlinked when it connects, and your choice about sharing chat history can't be changed afterwards. Keep the app open while the sync runs.

If Meta's popup shows an error
  • "Switching partners is not supported in this flow" (2655093), "This phone number is already registered with the product" (3441049), "Please disconnect your business platform to continue" (2655094): disconnect the previous provider in the WhatsApp Business app (Settings → Account → Business Platform), wait 15 minutes, and start again.
  • "The phone number you entered is already registered" (3441030): the number is still registered with another provider's API. De-register it first (how).
  • "This number is registered to an existing WhatsApp account": the signup treated it as a new number. Pick the option to connect your existing WhatsApp Business app.
  • "Your phone number isn't eligible to connect to the WhatsApp Business Platform" (3441045): use the WhatsApp Business app for a while, then try again.
  • "Your phone number isn't registered with WhatsApp Business" (2655095, 2655048): the number has to be set up on the WhatsApp Business app first.
