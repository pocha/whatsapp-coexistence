              De-register this number
              

                

                  Takes the number off the WhatsApp API, so another provider can onboard it. It stops
                  sending and receiving here. It does not delete the number or its message history,
                  and needs no PIN. For a coexistence number this isn't possible: Meta refuses it, and the
                  way off is in the WhatsApp Business app (Settings → Account → Business Platform → Disconnect).
                  Meta allows 10 attempts per number in 72 hours.
