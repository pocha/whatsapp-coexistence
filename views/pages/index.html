Why Coexistence?

Normally you have to pick one: the WhatsApp Business App (for humans on the phone) or the API (for automation). Coexistence lets the same number run both at once — Meta keeps them in sync, not us.

📥 A customer messages you

Delivered to both surfaces at once — it shows up in the phone app and fires a webhook to your API, the same instant.

📤 Your API sends a message

Automatically mirrored into the phone app's chat view. Meta's own docs: "Messages sent and received are mirrored between the Cloud API and WhatsApp Business app." Nothing extra to build.

💬 A human replies from the phone

This direction isn't automatic on your side — Meta fires an smb_message_echoes webhook event, and your app has to handle it (so your bot doesn't auto-reply on top of a human).

WhatsApp API Access in 3 Steps

PRE-REQUISITES

  • The WhatsApp Business app installed, with your phone number registered on it.
  • An app (a chatbot, for example) that receives incoming messages at a publicly hosted HTTPS endpoint. Ignore if you need API only for outgoing messages. If you do not have app yet, you can setup a test-chatbot

STEP 1

Connect your number & activate Coexistence

Open the Dashboard and create your Watobot API key and connect your WhatsApp Business app number through Meta's popup. We show the key once, so save it: we keep only a hash of it.

STEP 2

Point us at your incoming-message endpoint

Give us your app's incoming-message API endpoint as the override target. Incoming messages then go directly from WhatsApp to your app — we're never in that path.

STEP 3

Send messages through the endpoint we provide

Update your app to send outgoing messages through our endpoint instead of directly. This is the only place we sit in the path.

WHY WE GATEKEEP OUTGOING MESSAGES

It's the one place we stay involved, purely so we can protect your number from getting banned — rate limiting and other anti-spam checks are (upcoming). Everything else — incoming messages — never touches us, and your Meta access token stays encrypted with a key only you hold.

Features

🔒 Your tokens, locked with your key

We store your WhatsApp access token encrypted with your Watobot API key, which we never keep, so we can't read it. It is only unlocked, in memory, while a request carrying your key is handled.

⚡ Low latency

Incoming messages go directly from WhatsApp to your app — we're never in that path, so there's no extra hop slowing you down.

📖 Open source

Every line is on GitHub and self-hostable. Nothing is a black box — read the code, fork it, run your own copy.

🚦 Rate limiting for outgoing (upcoming)

Automatic rate limits on outgoing messages, so a misbehaving integration doesn't get your number banned.

📬 Per-recipient serialization (upcoming)

Messages to the same recipient get serialized, so retries or bugs on your end can't look like spam to WhatsApp.

  • Marketing template (promotions): the highest rate.
  • Utility template (order updates, reminders): a lower rate.
