Features

smart_toy

AI native onboarding

Add Watobot to Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code and ask it to onboard your number. It checks your setup and walks you through Meta's sign-up. How to connect

lock

Zero-knowledge encrypted token

Your WhatsApp access token is encrypted with your Watobot API key. We keep only a hash that can't open it, so even we can't read your token, and a leaked database gives an attacker nothing.

speed

Rate limits that protect your number

WhatsApp flags numbers that suddenly send far more than before. We limit template messages per minute, hour and day, and let each window grow only a little over the last. A blocked message gets a clear 429 and can always be retried. We don't queue messages yet.

content_copy

Template migration

Moving over? We copy your approved templates from your previous Messaging account, or import an export from your provider.

exit_to_app

Moving out of your previous provider

Guides for leaving your current provider with your number: what to export, how to de-register, what to expect. Read the guide

fact_check

Coexistence best practices, enforced

Meta lets each history and contacts sync run once. Before onboarding we check that your callback URL answers Meta and receives those events, so you don't lose the history you want to store.

monitor_heart

Sending stops when quality drops (upcoming)

Numbers often get blocked because the provider never tracks the quality of the number or its templates. We do, and we stop sends while it is degraded. The API response says why.

Your total cost: how Meta bills you

On top of Watobot's price you pay Meta directly, so attach a payment method to your Meta account. Meta charges per delivered message, by the customer's country, and never for messages customers send you.

  • Marketing template (promotions): the highest rate.
  • Utility template (order updates, reminders): a lower rate.
  • Authentication / service (login code templates; free-form replies after your free monthly quota): Meta lists these as separate categories, but they have exactly the same rate as utility in every country.
